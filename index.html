Tao (Jonas) Chen

Provost's Chair Professor in Finance · Nanyang Business School · Nanyang Technological University, Singapore

I study how financial market participants shape corporate policy, especially environmental, social and governance choices, and how FinTech and AI change financial inclusion and decision-making. My work appears in the Journal of Financial Economics, Management Science, JFQA, Review of Finance, Strategic Management Journal and Nature Climate Change.

I serve as Associate Editor of Financial Management, Journal of Business Research and The British Accounting Review, and as Vice President (Programs) of the Asian Finance Association. At NTU I am Academic Director of the MSc Finance programme and PhD Programme Coordinator.
