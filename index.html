About

I am Provost's Chair Professor in Finance at Nanyang Business School, Nanyang Technological University. My research covers sustainable finance and ESG, FinTech and AI, corporate innovation and corporate governance, and has appeared in the Journal of Financial Economics, Management Science, JFQA, Review of Finance, Strategic Management Journal and Nature Climate Change.

I serve as Associate Editor of Financial Management, Journal of Business Research and The British Accounting Review, and as Vice President (Programs) of the Asian Finance Association. At NTU I direct the MSc Finance programme and coordinate the Finance PhD programme.

I received my PhD in Finance from the Chinese University of Hong Kong, an MPhil from HKUST and a BA from Beijing Foreign Studies University.
