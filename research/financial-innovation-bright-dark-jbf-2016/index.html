Financial Innovation: The Bright and the Dark Sides

Thorsten Beck, Tao Chen, Chen Lin and Frank M. Song

Journal of Banking & Finance 72: 28–51, 2016

Publisher version (DOI 10.1016/j.jbankfin.2016.06.012) · Google Scholar

Main finding

Financial innovation is associated with faster growth in industries that rely on it, but also with greater bank fragility and larger losses during the 2007–08 crisis.
